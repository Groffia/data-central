Sample data for design review.

01 · What

Turn the dashboard into a decision

Interpretation turns analysis and visuals into business decisions: reviewing dashboards, drawing conclusions, recommending actions and telling the story. Business analysts and executives own it.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

02 · How

Notice, compare, look ahead

The reader notices what is off, compares it with the rest, and checks the forecast. Then they write one conclusion. Sample: Clinic B’s wait is 45 minutes and rising.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

03 · Why

A number alone decides nothing

Charts show what is true. A decision needs someone to say what it means and what to do. Without this step, the work of Stages 1 to 7 changes nothing.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

04 · When

When a decision is waiting

Whenever a dashboard shows something that needs a response, such as a number outside its range or a forecast that misses the goal.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

05 · Next

Weigh the options

A conclusion says what is wrong. Next: what could be done about it, and what each option would likely change.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

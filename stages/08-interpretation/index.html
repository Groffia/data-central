Sample data for design review.

01 · What

Turn the dashboard into a decision

Interpretation turns analysis and visuals into business decisions: reviewing dashboards, drawing conclusions, recommending actions and telling the story. Business analysts and executives own it.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

02 · How

Notice, compare, look ahead

The reader notices what is off, compares it with the rest, and checks the forecast. Then they write one conclusion. Sample: Clinic B’s wait is 45 minutes and rising.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

03 · Why

A number alone decides nothing

Charts show what is true. A decision needs someone to say what it means and what to do. Without this step, the work of Stages 1 to 7 changes nothing.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

04 · When

When a decision is waiting

Whenever a dashboard shows something that needs a response, such as a number outside its range or a forecast that misses the goal.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

05 · Next

Weigh the options

A conclusion says what is wrong. Next: what could be done about it, and what each option would likely change.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

01 · What

Compare what each action would change

Before any money is spent, the options are put side by side: what each would do to the problem, and what it would cost. Business analysts and executives weigh them.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with what-if features, such as Power BI.

02 · How

Simulate each option, then weigh the cost

Each option gets an estimate of the result. Then people set that against the cost. Sample: three options for Clinic B, each with a December wait and a monthly cost.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with what-if features, such as Power BI.

03 · Why

Money and staff are limited

The best-looking number may not be worth its cost. Comparing with and without an action shows what each one buys.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with what-if features, such as Power BI.

04 · When

Before anyone commits resources

After a conclusion says something must change, and before an option is chosen.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with what-if features, such as Power BI.

05 · Next

Tell the story

Options are weighed. Next: how the choice is explained to the people who must approve it, and who signs off.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with what-if features, such as Power BI.

01 · What

Explain it, then decide

The recommended option is told as a short story for the people who approve it, and one of them makes the call. Business analysts tell it. Executives decide.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with narrative summaries, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

02 · How

Draft the story, then an executive decides

The story covers what we saw, what we recommend, what to expect and what it costs. The executive approves, changes or declines, and the choice is recorded.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with narrative summaries, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

03 · Why

Every decision needs an owner

Where accuracy must be total, a person has to stand behind the choice. A recorded decision can be traced and questioned later.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with narrative summaries, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

04 · When

When an option is ready for approval

After the options are weighed, and before any money or staff are committed.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with narrative summaries, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

05 · Next

Act and close the loop

Once a person says yes, the action has to happen, and the next month’s data shows whether it worked.

Tools: Dashboard and BI tools with narrative summaries, such as Power BI and Databricks AI/BI.

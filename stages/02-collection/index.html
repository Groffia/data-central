Stage 2 of 8 · Data collection

Where data is gathered.

Collection brings data in from many places without losing any of it. At Tamsworth Health, the vitals row from Stage 1 meets lab results and patient feedback, and lands in a raw copy called Bronze.

Sample data for design review.

01 · What

Gather from many places

Tamsworth needs vitals, lab results and patient feedback together. Business stakeholders ask; data engineers connect each source.

Tools: FHIR APIs for the EHR; HL7 feeds from the lab system; Qualtrics or SurveyMonkey for surveys.

02 · How

Extract, then match

Each source is read in its own format, then matched on patient and time into one row.

Tools: Fivetran or Airbyte connectors; Apache Spark for the matching.

03 · Why

Nothing may be lost

Four things threaten that: volume, velocity, variety and veracity. Anything lost here is gone for good.

Tools: Apache Kafka or Azure Event Hubs for velocity; cloud object storage such as Amazon S3 for volume.

04 · When

Every 15 minutes, as received

The row lands unchanged in Bronze, a raw copy that can be replayed.

Tools: Apache Airflow, Databricks Lakeflow Jobs or Microsoft Fabric Data Factory; Delta Lake for Bronze.

05 · Next

Processing

Stage 3 cleans and standardizes the raw row. Collection never edits it.

Tools: dbt or Apache Spark, reading from Bronze.

01 · Where

Two places AI helps

At the start, where someone decides what to collect. At extraction, where messy inputs are read.

02 · What

It reads and flags repeats

A model turns survey text into fields. It flags repeated records so they stay out of the merge.

Tools: LLMs for unstructured extraction; Apache Spark or dbt for deduplication.

03 · Why it matters

Repeats stop here

A repeat is caught before it is counted twice. Bronze still holds exactly what arrived.

04 · Why it scales

Every record, every source

People can check a sample. An agent reads every record, all day. People see only what it flags.

05 · Next

People approve the plan

An agent can propose a new source to collect. A business stakeholder decides. Approved data flows on to Stage 3.
